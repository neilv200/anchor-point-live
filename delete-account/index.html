Delete your account

Anchored in Archery · by Neil Evan Valdez

Delete it in the app

  1. Open Anchored in Archery and go to the Account tab.
  2. Under Your account, tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. Your account and everything synced to it are deleted right away.

Or ask us by email

Email support@anchoredinarchery.com from the address you sign in with and ask us to delete your account. We'll delete it within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in email and account.
  • Your synced rounds, scores, arrow positions, heart rate readings, gear setups, training logs, coach links and notes, badges and leaderboard entries.
  • Your AI coach usage records.

What may be kept

  • Scores already posted to a tournament may stay in that event's public results, because they're part of the event record.
  • Purchase records kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat under their own policies. Cancel an active subscription in your Apple ID or Google Play settings; deleting your account doesn't cancel it.
  • Server backups are overwritten within 30 days.

Delete only some data

You can delete single rounds on the Stats tab without deleting your account, and turn off heart rate in Account at any time. Data saved only on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
