Privacy Policy

Anchored In Archery · Last updated November 9, 2026

Anchored In Archery is made by Neil Evan Valdez ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, and the choices you have. We don't sell your data, and we don't use it to track you across other apps or websites.

Using the app without an account

You can score, use the gear tools and train without signing in. That information stays on your device.

What we collect when you sign in

  • Email address, to create your account and sign you in.
  • Your archery data: rounds and scores, gear and setups, notes, goals and badges, so they sync between your devices and stay backed up.
  • Photos you choose to upload, such as a scorecard for a badge claim or an event flyer to scan.
  • Purchase status for Anchored Pro (from Apple or Google, through RevenueCat). We never see your card details.
  • Referral code you entered or own, and counts of sign-ups and Pro purchases made with it, for shop and team-shooter rewards.
  • AI coach usage counts, to apply daily limits.

Things you share on purpose

  • Leaderboards: your display name and scores, if you choose to post them.
  • Tournaments: the director enters archers' names, divisions and scores, which appear on a public leaderboard page and on TV screens at the event.
  • Coach connect: if you link to a coach, they see your saved rounds (scores, arrow positions and setup names) and the name you chose, until you stop sharing. Coaches can send you assignments and notes.

Location and microphone

  • Location is used only when you tap "Use my location" on the Range tab, to get local weather and elevation. Your approximate position is sent to the Open-Meteo weather service for that lookup and is not stored by us.
  • Microphone is used only by the sound chronograph and shot-noise tools. Audio is processed on your device and never uploaded.

Heart rate (Apple Health and Health Connect)

  • If you turn on Heart rate from your watch (Account), the app reads only your heart rate from Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), for the time you were scoring a round. It never reads other health data and never writes to your health data.
  • The heart rate for each end (average and peak) is saved with that round and synced to your account like the rest of the round. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone except as described here.
  • It is sent to the AI coach only if you also turn on Let the AI coach see my heart rate. A coach you link to can see it with your rounds while you share them.
  • You can turn heart rate off at any time in Account, and remove access in the Health app (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android). Deleting a round or your account deletes its heart rate too.

Services that process data for us

  • Supabase stores accounts and synced data.
  • Anthropic generates AI coach answers. Your question and the archery details needed to answer it (such as recent scores and your setup, and heart rate only if you allowed it) are sent to produce the reply.
  • RevenueCat, Apple and Google handle subscriptions.
  • Open-Meteo provides weather when you ask for it.

Each only uses the data to provide its service to us.

Children

Many young archers use the app through JOAD and youth programs. Archers under 13 should use the app with a parent or guardian, who should create and manage any account. We don't knowingly collect personal information from a child under 13 without a parent's consent; if you believe we have, contact us and we'll delete it. Under-18 archers need a parent or guardian's OK to share rounds with a coach, and coaches see only the name the archer chose.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data while your account is open. You can delete your account and its synced data at any time in the app (Account → Delete account), or by emailing us. Scores already posted to a tournament may stay in that event's results.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections, and the database only lets each person read their own data unless they chose to share it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and note important changes in the app.
