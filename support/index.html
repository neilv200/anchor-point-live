Anchor Point Archery Support

Scoring, gear tuning and coaching for every archer.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea? Email neilvaldez23@yahoo.com and include your phone model and what you were doing. We usually answer within a day or two.

Quick answers

  • Sign-in code didn't arrive? Check spam, wait a minute and tap Send again.
  • Restore Pro on a new phone: Account → Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.
  • Cancel Pro: in your Apple ID or Google Play subscriptions.
  • Delete your account: Account → Delete account.
  • Scoring a tournament bale: Score → Tournament day → Score a bale, then enter the code from the director.
  • Share rounds with your coach: Train → My coach → Add my coach.
